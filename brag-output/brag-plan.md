# Brag Plan: Blur Glass

## What is this app?
Blur Glass is an on-device macOS privacy app that ensures your screen is readable only by you: the moment you look away, leave the frame, or a second face enters the room, a frosted glass shield covers your entire desktop.

## The angle
The absolute superpower of working anywhere—coffee shops, flights, open offices—without anyone glancing at your work. Not a tiny privacy screen filter, but an intelligent, hardware-accelerated Apple Vision shield that blanks the entire OS the microsecond someone peeks.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
"Your screen is for your eyes only." High-contrast, sleek typography on a dark glass macOS backdrop, transitioning immediately into the live macOS desktop environment.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The macOS frosted glass control popover activating with the signature Iris aperture mark and system toggle.
- The shoulder surfer moment: someone walks up or peers over your shoulder, status flashes "Extra face detected — Screen blurred", and the entire desktop frosts into a deep Apple-style blur instantly.
- The on-device privacy guarantee: Apple Vision + Keychain-encrypted biometric templates + 0 cloud telemetry.

## Outro / punchline
The glowing Iris aperture mark locks in: "Blur Glass. Screen privacy, naturally."

## User flow worth showing
1. Active Protection: Owner sits in front of Mac -> green dot glows "Screen clear".
2. Intruder Event: Second face appears in camera frame -> status turns red "Extra face detected — Screen blurred", full screen blurs immediately.
3. Clean Dismissal: Stranger leaves / owner looks back -> screen clears smoothly.
