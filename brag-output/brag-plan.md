# Brag Plan: Blur Glass (Cinematic Human Edition)

## What is this app?
Blur Glass is an on-device macOS privacy app that ensures your screen is readable only by you: the moment you look away, leave the frame, or a second face enters the room, an instant frosted glass shield covers the entire desktop.

## The angle
Cinematic thriller meets Apple product film: showcasing the real-world tension of working in public spaces (cafes, airports, open offices) where curious eyes and shoulder surfers peek at your confidential code and financials—and how Blur Glass uses on-device Apple Vision to instantly frost the display before they can see a single pixel.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Cinematic shot of a professional working on a laptop in a moody cafe:
"EVERY DAY, YOU WORK IN PUBLIC. PASSWORDS. FINANCIALS. CODE."

## Key moments (the middle)
- The human threat: an over-the-shoulder shot of a stranger leaning in behind the developer to spy on her screen.
- The on-device Vision lock: Neural Engine face detection bounding box brackets the intruder in red: `[ ⚠ EXTRA FACE DETECTED ]`.
- The Instant Frost Defense: The laptop screen instantly turns into a dense, unreadable frosted glass blur, while the Blur Glass macOS popover shows active protection.
- 100% On-device privacy architecture: Apple Vision, Keychain encryption, zero cloud telemetry.

## Outro / punchline
The glowing Aperture Iris brand mark slams in on the music drop:
"BLUR GLASS. Screen privacy, naturally."

## User flow worth showing
1. Owner working peacefully in public -> owner face tracked.
2. Threat arrives -> stranger leans in over shoulder -> Vision detection triggers in 150ms.
3. Instant screen frost -> entire desktop blurs into heavy frosted glass -> stranger sees nothing.

- Interpretation: Epic pacing, slow cinematic dolly pushes, bold letterboxed typography, real human emotion and tension, high-stakes payoff.
