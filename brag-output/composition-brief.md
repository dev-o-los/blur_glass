# Hyperframes Composition Brief: Blur Glass (Cinematic Human Edition)

## Objective
Create a cinematic, trailer-style launch video for Blur Glass featuring real humans in authentic workspace settings.

## Output
- Composition directory: `brag-output/composition/`
- Rendered video: `brag-output/brag.mp4`
- Format: landscape — 1920x1080
- Duration: 19.5s

## Creative Direction
- Tone preset: cinematic
- Creative direction: Dramatic trailer-scale privacy film featuring real humans in public workspaces.
- Human footage / imagery:
  - `assets/images/owner_cafe.jpg`: Focused developer working in a moody ambient cafe.
  - `assets/images/shoulder_surfer.jpg`: Tense over-the-shoulder shot of a stranger leaning in to peek at the laptop display.
  - `assets/images/frosted_screen.jpg`: The laptop screen instantly turning into a thick, frosted glass blur.
- Tech elements:
  - Apple Vision HUD biometric bounding box tracking overlay
  - Real Blur Glass frosted glass macOS popover card with Aperture Iris mark
  - Real status transitions: "Screen clear" -> "Extra face detected — Screen blurred"
  - Hardware specs: Apple Vision Engine, Encrypted Keychain, 0 Cloud Telemetry.

## Storyboard
1. Scene 1 (0.00s - 4.39s): Developer working in public cafe. "EVERY DAY, YOU WORK IN PUBLIC. PASSWORDS. FINANCIALS. CODE."
2. Scene 2 (4.39s - 8.74s): Suspicious stranger leans in behind her over the shoulder. Vision HUD targets intruder. "UNTIL SOMEONE LOOKS OVER YOUR SHOULDER."
3. Scene 3 (8.74s - 13.11s) [Beat-locked 8.74s]: The instant they peek, the laptop screen blurs into frosted glass with the Blur Glass active protection card. "THE INSTANT THEY PEEK, YOUR SCREEN BLURS."
4. Scene 4 (13.11s - 16.93s) [Beat-locked 13.11s]: 100% on-device architecture. 3 security badges arrive on the beat grid.
5. Scene 5 (16.93s - 19.50s) [Beat-locked 17.47s]: Glowing Aperture Iris logo slam. "BLUR GLASS — Screen privacy, naturally."
