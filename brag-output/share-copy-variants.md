## LinkedIn (Professional & Product-focused)
Working in public spaces like cafes, flights, and co-working areas often forces a compromise between productivity and privacy.

We built Blur Glass for macOS. The moment you look away, leave the desk, or an extra face enters the frame, a hardware-accelerated frosted glass shield blankets the entire desktop—Safari, terminal, and all Spaces.

Everything runs on-device using Apple's Vision framework, with owner biometric templates encrypted in Keychain. Zero telemetry, zero cloud dependencies.

## GitHub / Discord / IndieHackers
Blur Glass: Screen privacy, naturally. 
An open-source macOS app that watches for shoulder surfers and automatically blurs the desktop whenever a stranger looks over or you look away.
Built with Flutter & native Swift (Vision framework + ScreenCaptureKit).
